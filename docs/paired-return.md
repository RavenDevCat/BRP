# Paired Scheduled Return Measurements

## Operator Workflow

1. In New Audit, upload and validate a China To-School workbook. Enable
   Scheduled Job, choose dates, and set Afternoon return measurement time
   (default 15:40, Asia/Shanghai). The morning trigger remains the existing
   operating-window start. Direct-to-School supports the same paired schedule.
2. Each selected date creates two independently executable jobs in one atomic
   submission. The pair retains an immutable original-plan fingerprint.
3. Audit History groups the pair. In audit details, switch Morning to school /
   Afternoon return. Direct-to-School has the equivalent history and tabs.
4. In Afternoon return, select All routes or Under 1 hour; select a route to
   inspect school-to-stop ride times. Export statistics or the visible map.

New Audit's morning job retains its existing solver, final-traffic checks and
audit outputs. Its afternoon job uses the dedicated return measurement engine,
not the optimizer or the audit's deep-verification/report pipeline. Immediate
audits, From-School uploads and non-China audits retain their single-job flow.

The afternoon plan is the original uploaded morning plan in reverse stop order,
not an optimized/removal proposal generated by the morning audit. Vehicles,
assignments and riders are retained. Zero-rider stops remain stops. Only the
explicit school becomes the start. Coordinates are unchanged; the system does
not invent opposite-side platforms for return journeys.

## Timing And Failure Policy

- Fresh AMap routing at execution time; no Google timing or geocoding requests
  are introduced by the return measurement. Google's rollout remains separate.
- Driving plus all non-school dwell is the whole-route total. Strictly less
  than 3,600 raw seconds is highlighted; exactly 3,600 is not.
- A student's ride ends upon arrival at their stop, before their own dwell.
- Each route records its sampling timestamp. This is a current-traffic snapshot,
  not a future forecast or an observed bus journey.
- Unavailable routes retain stop and rider counts and remain non-passing.
  Partial coverage remains explicit in results and exports.
- New pairs have a 15-minute trigger grace period. An expired job fails instead
  of claiming later traffic is the scheduled sample. PM cannot be released early.
- AM failure or cancellation does not cancel PM. Actions target the selected
  period only. A remaining sibling stays accessible if one period is deleted.
- Submission identity, owner, queue scope and date provide idempotency. Both
  records insert in one transaction; conflicting settings are rejected.
- Existing schedules are not automatically backfilled. Historical results are
  not rewritten and past afternoon traffic cannot be recreated.

## Acceptance Contract

- Exercise New Audit upload, schedule controls, actual pair submission, grouped
  history, both detail tabs, maps, short-route filtering and both export formats.
- Verify immediate and non-China flows, unchanged morning dispatch, dedicated
  PM dispatch, cancellation independence, access control and expiry/early gates.
- Test duplicate/concurrent requests, transaction rollback, original-plan
  preservation, zero-rider stops and the exact 3,600-second boundary.
- Verify desktop/mobile layouts and build the frontend after committing so
  the deployed version marker identifies the tested source revision.
- Recorded-response replay validates execution and rendering, not new traffic.
  Keep replay evidence and fresh provider measurements explicitly separate.
- Use isolated acceptance storage. Preserve production schedules, quota counters,
  caches and historical results. Production promotion requires separate approval.
